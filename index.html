ttrpg-engine

A headless, system-agnostic TTRPG engine. Frontends dispatch a typed Intent; the engine applies validated math and emits a minimal RFC 6902 JSON Patch delta — consumed identically by a Discord bot, a 2D web app, or a cinematic 3D virtual tabletop.
